About and help

What IskolarPH does

IskolarPH collects scholarship information in one place so you can search it, compare requirements, and keep track of your own applications.

How to use it

  1. Browse or search the directory and open a scholarship for details.
  2. Use the eligibility matcher to see which ones may fit you.
  3. Save scholarships, then manage them in the tracker.

Where the information comes from

Records are written from public announcements, official pages, and a shared scholarship list used as a reference. Each record shows a last-checked date. Deadlines that were not confirmed are labeled "Needs Verification".

Limits you should know

  • The matcher compares your answers with recorded rules. It cannot promise you will qualify.
  • Deadlines can change. Always confirm on the official page before applying.
  • Your saved items live in this browser only. They do not sync across devices.

Found something outdated?

Open any scholarship and use "Report outdated info". In this version, reports are not sent anywhere.
